# avocadoMini Mini200 E2 — four-tower integration baseline

2026-09-21 / owner direction / Material Invention · avocadoMini / `ROCK`

## Adopted product form

The owner confirmed the public and product exterior after reviewing the E2 OS and hardware documents: **avocadoMini keeps four silver telescoping Motion Towers surrounding one low central unit**. The image currently used by the product Site is the visual authority for arrangement and silhouette.

The compact `198 × 178 × 198 mm` three-window enclosure in the supplied Mini200 E2 hardware document is therefore **not the adopted exterior of the complete avocadoMini product**. It remains a mechanical study and source of candidate assumptions. Do not replace the four-tower image with a small box or state that the public product is a single-box console.

## E2 architecture applied to this form

The central unit is the integration point for the E2 architecture:

- RockstarOS Shell, Broker, executor and capability-adapter boundaries;
- local game loop and versioned spatial input;
- push-to-talk Japanese speech recognition, final-intent execution and cancellation;
- physical microphone off, camera consent, default capture off and recovery behavior;
- provider-independent AI adapters, asset lineage, rights, cost and delivery receipts;
- candidate 32 GiB shared memory and 1 TB NVMe storage budgets;
- 1080p / 60 fps, 32 virtual objects and body-to-display P95 at or below 100 ms as design targets;
- signed OS image, recovery, update and manufacturing gates kept separate from Web previews.

The exact distribution of compute, synchronization, power and safety control between the four towers and central unit is not resolved by the current E2 documents. The earlier P0.2 tower dimensions and safety behavior remain the current mechanical reference for the towers until a combined E2 integration package replaces them.

## Evidence boundaries

- The supplied `RockstarOS_Mini200_E2_OS_Design` and `avocadoMini_Mini200_E2_Hardware_Design` files are design references. Their PDF/DOCX originals are not copied into the public repository.
- Reference-model checks are not real-device, ASR, thermal, acoustic, optical or manufacturing acceptance.
- AMD Ryzen AI 9 HX 370, the camera modules and the microphone array are candidates, not approved production parts.
- GTA compatibility, Rockstar Games partnership scope, satellite reception and a general RockstarOS installer are not proven by these documents.
- The four-tower rendering is concept art, not a photograph of built hardware.

## Public presentation

The product Site keeps the owner-confirmed four-tower exterior and explains Mini200 E2 as the central system behind it. Public copy distinguishes design targets from measured performance, keeps payments closed until the commercial terms are complete, and keeps the OS installer in status-only mode until signed images and recovery evidence exist.

## Next engineering package

The next package must combine the accepted exterior and E2 architecture in one traceable set:

1. central-unit envelope, service access, board choice, power and cooling;
2. four-tower data, clock, power and safety wiring;
3. sensor-to-body-input calibration and latency budget;
4. physical privacy switching and reconnect defaults;
5. signed RockstarOS media, factory recovery and update rollback;
6. bench, full-system, thermal, acoustic and manufacturing acceptance records.

Until those items pass, this is an integrated product direction and public design baseline, not manufacturing approval or a completed product.

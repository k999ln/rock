4 MOTION TOWERS + 1 EDGE HUB / CG BASED ON THE P0.2 DESIGN

01 / SPATIAL SENSING

See what changes,
right where you work.

The cap covers the lenses while idle and lifts at startup. Three upper cameras sit in a horizontal row and scan the area ahead in sequence.

UPPER / P0.2Three horizontally aligned cameras

At startup, the cameras scan the forward 180° in sequence. The cap physically covers the lenses while idle.

LOWER / CONCEPTLower sensor window

An additional concept for viewing the installation surface from near floor level. Its configuration and performance are not yet validated.

The blue light is a CG effect that communicates viewing direction and active state

02 / ADAPTIVE HEIGHT

A viewpoint that
adapts to the work.

It extends from 850 mm stored to 1,200 mm freestanding. It reaches 1,800 mm only after detecting a dock or floor latch, aligning its view with the work position.

850 / 1,200 / 1,800 mm · P0 DESIGN VALUES

03 / INDEPENDENT SAFETY

Safety supports
every movement.

Independent safety controls check contact across three feet, tilt, and dock state. A freestanding fault triggers a slow retraction; a docked fault stops motion.

Ø220 mm BASE / Ø520 mm FOOT ENVELOPE

04 / EDGE HUB + ROCKSTAROS

Four data streams.
One spatial experience.

Four towers connect to the Edge Hub over wired PoE. AR output goes to glasses, tablets, or a PC. The RockstarOS LLM is designed to guide and explain the work.

P0.2 HAS NO BATTERY INSIDE THE TOWERS / THE LLM CANNOT AUTHORIZE MOTION, RECORDING, OR SAFETY OVERRIDES
